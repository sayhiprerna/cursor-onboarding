# LinkedIn Organic Content Strategy for B2B SaaS

## Project Overview

This repository contains research on leading practitioners, marketers, founders, and strategists whose work helps explain how LinkedIn drives awareness, demand generation, positioning, thought leadership, and growth for B2B SaaS companies.

The goal of this project is to identify high-signal experts, collect examples of their recent content, and organize their ideas into a research base that can later be used to develop a practical LinkedIn playbook for B2B SaaS organizations.

---

## Research Objective

To understand how successful B2B SaaS companies and individuals use LinkedIn organic content to:

* Build audience and trust
* Generate demand
* Establish thought leadership
* Improve positioning and differentiation
* Grow founder and executive visibility
* Increase content reach and distribution

* `other/` contains supporting material from podcasts, interviews, newsletters, books, and additional sources.

---

## Key Learnings

* LinkedIn content performs best when it provides value directly on the platform.
* Distribution is often as important as content creation.
* Strong positioning improves content effectiveness.
* Founder-led content can function as a go-to-market channel.
* Category creation and executive thought leadership can accelerate B2B SaaS growth.
* Consistency, clarity, and audience understanding are recurring themes across experts.
